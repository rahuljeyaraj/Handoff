Cell voltage is the VSYS/3 reading on GP29 plus about 0.35 V for D1's drop. Thresholds are coarse on purpose: a Li-ion curve is almost flat between 3.7 and 3.9 V, so a percentage would be fiction. Unknown is its own state and must never render as Critical.
USB power is not charging. The TP4056 is off-board on J5 and the app cannot see it. USB into the Pico only carries the board — VSYS then reads about 4.6 V, which is how you detect it, and the cell is unmeasurable because D1 blocks it.
